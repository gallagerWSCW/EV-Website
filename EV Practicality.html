EV PRACTICALITY

The Reality of EV Range

EV range has improved rapidly over the
past decade. Early models could only travel
100–150 km per charge, but most modern EVs
now exceed 300 km, with many reaching over
500 km.

Most New Zealanders drive less than 40 km per day,
meaning EVs can last several days on a single charge
and can be easily recharged overnight at home.
