Why is Carbon Emission a problem in New Zealand

Climate change is a serious issue that affects everyone in New Zealand. Over the last century, the country's average temperature has risen. According to NIWA's Seven Station Temperature Series, New Zealand's annual temperature increased by 1.09°C between 1909 and 2018. One of the main causes is the large amount of carbon emissions produced in the country. In 2022, New Zealand emitted about 32.4 million tonnes of fossil CO₂, and each person produced an average of 6.31 tonnes of CO₂. This places New Zealand among the countries with relatively high carbon emissions per person.
