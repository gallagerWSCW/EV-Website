CARBON EMISSION

Carbon emissions are one of the main causes of climate change, and transport is a significant contributor in New Zealand. Most petrol and diesel vehicles release carbon dioxide (CO₂) into the atmosphere, where it traps heat and contributes to rising global temperatures. These changes increase the risk of extreme weather events, sea-level rise, and other environmental impacts that affect communities across New Zealand.

EV BENEFITS

Electric vehicles (EVs) offer significant environmental benefits by producing far fewer carbon emissions than petrol or diesel vehicles. Unlike conventional vehicles that burn fossil fuels, EVs run on electricity, helping reduce greenhouse gas emissions from the transport sector. In New Zealand, this advantage is even greater because around 85% of the country's electricity is generated from renewable sources such as hydroelectric, geothermal, and wind power.

EV PRACTICALITY

Modern electric vehicles are practical for everyday driving and even longer trips around New Zealand. Many EVs can travel over 400–600 km on a single charge, which is enough for most daily commutes and many intercity journeys. Drivers can conveniently charge their vehicles at home overnight or use public fast chargers during longer trips.

COST SAVINGS

Electric vehicles can significantly reduce the cost of everyday driving. Charging an EV at home is usually much cheaper than buying petrol or diesel, resulting in lower running costs per 100 km.
